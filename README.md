## The entry (128 characters)

> Sorry, I can't make it — I already sat through this exact meeting in 2019, 2021, and last Tuesday, and the ending never changes.

The excuse skips the stock alibis (bad wifi, a cat, a dentist) and goes for the meeting itself: the speaker has already sat through this exact meeting in 2019, in 2021, and last Tuesday, and the ending never changes. Skipping it is not dodging work — it is refusing to rewatch a meeting whose outcome is already known. The laugh lands with a nod of recognition from anyone who has watched the same agenda come back, year after year, with nothing ever decided.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only, no network, no local imports) writes the single line above to `/output/entry.txt` — exactly 128 characters, UTF-8, no trailing newline.

## Run

```sh
python3 -P -s main.py
```

The entry is written to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set).
